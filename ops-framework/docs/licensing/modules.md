## What each module is

### `CORE` - Runs, findings, exceptions, alerts, check runner, extensions

The store of runs and findings, the exceptions app, the alert router and its channels, run lock and abort, the configuration apps and their gate, the generic check runner with the check registry and the business process register, parameters, retention purge, the process catalogue model with its product baseline S4CORE, explainable results, the customer extension contract and the licence itself. Always included - never refused, so a customer whose licence lapsed still sees and works its existing exceptions.

- Entry points: `src/zcl_ca_ops_store.clas.abap`, `src/zcl_ca_ops_alert_router.clas.abap`, `src/zcl_ca_ops_check_runner.clas.abap`, `src/zca_ops_check_run.prog.abap`, `src/zca_ops_retention.prog.abap`, `src/zcl_ca_ops_catalog.clas.abap`, `src/ui/zui_ca_ops_exc.srvd.srvdsrv`, `src/ui/zui_ca_ops_cfg.srvd.srvdsrv`, `src/ui/zui_ca_ops_dash.srvd.srvdsrv`, `src/zca_ops_license_install.prog.abap`, `src/ui/zui_ca_ops_lic.srvd.srvdsrv`, `config/extension_points.json`, `tools/extensions.py`
- Registered check classes: `ZCL_CA_OPS_CHK_LICENSE`
- Documents: `README.md`, `docs/EXPLAINABLE_RESULTS.md`, `docs/EXTENDING_WITH_CUSTOMER_DATA.md`, `docs/LICENSING.md`

### `CONTROL` - Control register and daily control review

Controls with their guidance and covered checks, the daily review with status per business date and company code, sign-off and reopen reasons.

- Entry points: `src/ui/zui_ca_ops_rev.srvd.srvdsrv`, `src/zcl_ca_ops_review_policy.clas.abap`
- Registered check classes: -
- Documents: `docs/CONTROL_REVIEW.md`

### `ONBOARD` - Process onboarding: no-code aggregate checks

No-code aggregate checks over approved tables (COUNT / AGE per company code, windows), the onboarding completeness check, the outage risk check over aggregate rows and the scaffold of a new business process.

- Entry points: `src/zcl_ca_ops_agg_query.clas.abap`, `../tools/abaptools/new_process.py`
- Registered check classes: `ZCL_CA_OPS_CHK_AGG`, `ZCL_CA_OPS_CHK_ONBOARD`, `ZCL_CA_OPS_CHK_OUTAGE`
- Documents: `docs/ADDING_A_BUSINESS_PROCESS.md`

### `JOBS` - SAP job monitor

Failed and late SAP jobs with their steps, job log, application log and business objects; scheduler jobs; the job failures board.

- Entry points: `src/zcl_ca_ops_job_monitor.clas.abap`, `src/ui/zui_ca_ops_jobs.srvd.srvdsrv`
- Registered check classes: `ZCL_CA_OPS_CHK_JOB_FAIL`, `ZCL_CA_OPS_CHK_JOB_STATE`
- Documents: `docs/JOB_MONITORING.md`

### `RESPONSE` - Operations response features

Runbook links, alert correlation, escalation until acknowledged, planned downtime windows, response-time metrics and the shift handover.

- Entry points: `src/zcl_ca_ops_correlator.clas.abap`, `src/zcl_ca_ops_downtime.clas.abap`, `src/ui/zui_ca_ops_dtw.srvd.srvdsrv`, `src/ui/zui_ca_ops_met.srvd.srvdsrv`, `src/ui/zui_ca_ops_hovr.srvd.srvdsrv`
- Registered check classes: -
- Documents: `docs/OPERATIONS_FEATURES.md`

### `LINKS` - Link registry: customer layers, walker, registry check

A customer's own layer of the link registry (custom tables, custom fields, own links, switch-off of standard links), the registry check and the generator outputs. The product layers S4CORE / FICA are content the other modules read and stay part of them.

- Entry points: `src/zcl_ca_ops_link_walker.clas.abap`, `src/zcl_ca_ops_link_registry.clas.abap`, `tools/link_registry.py`
- Registered check classes: `ZCL_CA_OPS_CHK_LINKS`
- Documents: `docs/LINK_REGISTRY.md`

The SAP FI-CA standard processes of the catalogue with their steps, tables, dependencies and monitoring templates.

- Entry points: `config/catalogue/packages/fica.json`
- Registered check classes: -
- Documents: `docs/PROCESS_CATALOGUE.md`

### `CATALOGUE_FSCD` - Process catalogue package FSCD (SAP FS-CD)

The SAP FS-CD standard processes of the catalogue (payment plans, premium, broker, coinsurance, commission, claims) with their monitoring templates.

- Entry points: `config/catalogue/packages/fscd.json`
- Registered check classes: -
- Documents: `docs/PROCESS_CATALOGUE.md`

### `CATALOGUE_FSRI` - Process catalogue: FS-RI integration

The reinsurance (FS-RI) process of the FS-CD package, licensed on its own.

- Entry points: `config/catalogue/packages/fscd.json`
- Registered check classes: -
- Documents: `docs/PROCESS_CATALOGUE.md`

### `PAYMODEL` - Payment model generator

The generator of a customer's payment model (adapter views, cube, trend, object pages, DCL, service) from the product templates. Its output is generated into the customer's own package, so the licence is enforced by contract and the version history of the generated objects, not by a runtime gate (docs/LICENSING.md).

- Entry points: `templates/payment_model`, `../tools/abaptools/payment_model.py`
- Registered check classes: -
- Documents: `docs/ADDING_A_CUSTOMER_PAYMENT_MODEL.md`

### `BREAKS` - Clearing-account break analysis and pilot pack

Break analysis of clearing accounts (buckets, rules, snapshots, list, cube, query), the unexplained-break check and the pilot pack (metrics, baselines, export).

- Entry points: `src/zca_ops_brk_run.prog.abap`, `src/ui/zui_ca_ops_brk.srvd.srvdsrv`, `src/ui/zui_ca_ops_pilot.srvd.srvdsrv`
- Registered check classes: `ZCL_CA_OPS_CHK_BREAK`
- Documents: `docs/BREAK_ANALYSIS.md`, `docs/PILOT_PACK.md`

### `FLOW` - Account flow reconciliation

Account flows by stage and role with movement, opening, closing and unexplained, the evaluation of what checks account for, tiles and the multidimensional query.

- Entry points: `src/zca_ops_flow_run.prog.abap`, `src/ui/zui_ca_ops_flow.srvd.srvdsrv`
- Registered check classes: -
- Documents: `docs/ACCOUNT_FLOW.md`

## Configuration whose module follows from the row

| Entity of `ZUI_CA_OPS_CFG` | Field | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Process | `cat_process` | a business process monitors a catalogue process: the module of that process's package (or of the process itself) |
| RegisteredCheck | `class_name` | a registered check runs a class of a module (check_classes below) |
| CatalogActivation | `process_id` | a customer activates a catalogue process: the module of its package |
| CatalogPackage | `pkg_id` | a catalogue package switched on: its module |
